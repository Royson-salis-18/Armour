ARMOR

Advanced Rider Monitoring & Orientation Reasoning System

Ride together. Come home together.

A safety net for group rides. Here is how it works, in under a minute.

See everyone live

One shared map shows where every rider is, who is ahead, who is behind and who has stopped.

Stop, chat, talk

The lead calls a team stop. Chat with the group, or just talk to ARMOR and it does the tapping for you.

Help comes by itself

After a crash, or a long stop with no answer, ARMOR texts your location and rings your contacts and the nearest rider.

Watch it work

Everyone rides together. ARMOR watches the group.

One rider stops, away from the others.

ARMOR asks: are you okay?

No answer. SOS texts, with the location, go to contacts and nearby riders.

The nearest rider is called and heads over. Help is on the way.

Get ready in three steps

  1. Allow location and notificationsWhen asked, so your group sees you and you see them.
  2. Add an emergency contactOn the Android app, they get your location by SMS if you go down.
  3. Create or join a rideMake a ride and share the 6-letter code, or enter a friend's code.
